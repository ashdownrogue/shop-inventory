# Shop Inventory Audit Checklist
**Purpose:** walk the shop with this open and mark every line. Output is a gap list you can buy from.
**Scope:** itemized to the individual socket, wrench, tap, nozzle, and connector.

---

## How to run this audit

**Time budget:** 90 to 120 minutes for a full pass. Do it in one sitting if you can; a half-finished audit is worse than none.

**Marking system:** check the box if you have it, and annotate the ones that need attention:

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[x]` | Have it, condition is fine |
| `[x] U` | Have it, but it is junk or worn and should be upgraded |
| `[x] 2` | Have it, quantity noted (use for consumables and clamps) |
| `[ ]` | Do not have it, candidate for the buy list |
| `[ ] !` | Do not have it, and it is blocking a job I want to do |
| `~~struck~~` | Not applicable to my work, permanently out of scope |

**Order of operations:**
1. Start at Section 1 (Safety). Anything unchecked there goes to the top of the buy list regardless of cost.
2. Empty one drawer at a time. Do not audit from memory; memory says you own three 10mm sockets and reality says zero.
3. As you go, pull anything broken or rusted into a pile by the door. Audit and purge in the same pass.
4. When done, fill in Section 16 (Gap Summary) and price only the `!` items first.
